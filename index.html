GoldGadai MY - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to GoldGadai MY ("GoldGadai MY", "the App", "we", "us", or "our"). GoldGadai MY is a Malaysia-focused application providing gold price information, currency rates and conversion information, gold pawn and Ar-Rahnu location information, and related news and informational content.

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information may be collected, used, shared, and protected when you use GoldGadai MY.

Important: GoldGadai MY does not require users to create an account or log in. The App does not ask users to provide their name, email address, telephone number, address, payment information, health information, contacts, photos, videos, or other personal profile information in order to use its core features.

2. Information Collected Automatically

GoldGadai MY uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to support the App through advertising. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain information from the device for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes.

The types of information may include:

  • Approximate location: The device IP address may be collected and may be used to estimate the general location of the device.
  • App activity: Information about interactions with the App, such as app launches, taps, and video views, may be collected.
  • Diagnostics: Information related to application and SDK performance may be collected, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.
  • Device or other identifiers: Identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, App Set ID, and, where applicable, other device or account-related identifiers may be collected.

According to Google's current Google Mobile Ads SDK documentation, these categories may be collected and shared automatically for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

3. How Information Is Used

Information collected through advertising services may be used for:

  • Advertising and marketing.
  • Analytics and measurement.
  • Fraud prevention, security, and compliance.
  • Supporting the operation, reliability, and performance of advertising services.

GoldGadai MY does not use the collected information to create a personal account or personal profile for users.

4. Data Sharing

GoldGadai MY does not sell users' personal information.

Information described in this Privacy Policy may be processed or shared with Google and its relevant advertising and technology services when required to provide advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance functions.

Google's own policies and documentation govern Google's handling of information processed through its services.

For more information about how Google uses information from apps and websites that use its services, please visit:

Google - How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

5. Advertising

GoldGadai MY uses Google AdMob for advertising. Advertising may involve the processing of device and application information as described in this Privacy Policy.

Google may provide controls that allow users to manage advertising identifiers or advertising-related settings through their Android device settings or other Google-provided controls.

Android Advertising ID controls may allow users to reset or delete their advertising ID, subject to the controls and functionality provided by the Android operating system and Google.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through GoldGadai MY and the services used by the App.

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect information within our control.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

GoldGadai MY does not create user accounts and does not maintain a personal user profile requiring account deletion.

GoldGadai MY does not maintain a separate database of users' names, email addresses, telephone numbers, payment information, or other personal profile information for account management.

Information processed by Google AdMob is handled according to Google's applicable policies, systems, retention practices, and user controls.

Because GoldGadai MY does not provide user account creation, there is no GoldGadai MY user account to delete.

8. Children's Privacy

GoldGadai MY is not designed as a children's application.
